This Privacy Policy explains how Everland Technology Limited ("Everland", "we", "us") collects, uses, shares, and protects your information when you use the Her Bible app (the "App"). By using the App you agree to this Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

Everland Technology Limited

2. Information we collect

2.1 Information you provide

  • Optional account info — if you choose to sign in, we receive your name, email address, and profile picture from your chosen provider (Google, Apple, or Facebook), or your email address for email‑link sign‑in.
  • Content you create — notes, reflections, highlights, bookmarks, saved verses, mood check‑ins, prayer completions, and your last‑read position.

2.2 Information collected automatically

  • Device & technical info — device model, operating‑system version, app version, language/region, and similar diagnostics.
  • Usage & analytics — in‑app events (e.g., screens viewed, features used, prayers completed) used to understand and improve the App.
  • Crash & performance data — anonymous diagnostics that help us fix bugs.
  • Advertising identifiers — on the free (ad‑supported) tier, the Google Advertising ID (Android) / Identifier for Advertisers (iOS) and related ad‑interaction data, used to serve and measure ads (see Section 5).

The App stores your content locally on your device by default and syncs it to your account only if you sign in.

3. How we use your information

  • To provide the App — your streak, notes, highlights, and reading position.
  • To sync your data across devices when you sign in.
  • To diagnose crashes, measure performance, and improve features.
  • To deliver and measure advertising on the free tier.
  • To comply with legal obligations and prevent fraud or abuse.

4. Third‑party services we use

We use the following service providers, which may process certain data on our behalf or as independent controllers:

  • Google AdMob — serves ads and processes advertising identifiers. See Google's "How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services" and the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Google Firebase (Analytics, Crashlytics, Authentication) — usage analytics, crash reporting, and sign‑in.
  • Sign‑in providers — Google, Apple, and Facebook, only if you choose to sign in with them.

We require our providers to protect your data and use it only for the purposes we specify.

5. Advertising & your choices

  • The free tier shows ads via Google AdMob. A one‑time in‑app purchase ("Remove Ads") disables them.
  • Personalized vs. non‑personalized ads. In the EEA, UK, and Switzerland we request your consent through Google's consent management (UMP) before serving personalized ads. Elsewhere, ads may be personalized by default subject to your device settings.
  • Your controls. You can reset or limit your advertising ID in your device settings (Android: Settings → Google → Ads; iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking / Apple Advertising). On iOS, App Tracking Transparency (ATT) governs cross‑app tracking.
  • We do not use your notes, highlights, or mood data to build advertising profiles, and we do not sell your personal data.

6. Legal bases (EEA/UK – GDPR)

  • Performance of a contract — to provide the App's core features.
  • Consent — for personalized advertising where required, and for optional sign‑in. You may withdraw consent at any time.
  • Legitimate interests — to secure, debug, and improve the App, balanced against your rights.
  • Legal obligation — to comply with applicable law.

7.2 EEA / UK / Switzerland (GDPR)

7.3 United States (California & other states)

7.4 Brazil (LGPD)

If you are in Brazil, the Lei Geral de Proteção de Dados (LGPD) gives you the rights to confirmation and access, correction, anonymization or deletion, portability, information about sharing, and to withdraw consent. Our processing relies on legal bases including consent, performance of a contract, and legitimate interest. You may contact us at admin@everlandapps.com or lodge a complaint with the Brazilian National Data Protection Authority (ANPD).

8. Children

The App is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13 (or under 16 in the EEA). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Data retention

We keep personal data only as long as needed for the purposes above or as required by law. If you delete your account or request deletion, we delete or anonymize your data except where retention is legally required.

10. Data security

We use appropriate technical and organizational measures, including encryption in transit, access controls, and regular review. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

11. International transfers

Your information may be processed outside your country, including in Hong Kong and wherever our service providers operate. Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards for such transfers.

12. Changes to this Policy
