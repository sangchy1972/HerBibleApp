Frequently asked questions

How do I keep my progress if I change phones?

Sign in (Profile → Sign in) with Google, Apple or Facebook before switching devices, then sign in with the same account on the new phone.

How do I change prayer reminder times?

Profile → Notifications lets you turn the morning and evening reminders on or off and set their times.

How do I change the app language?

Profile → Language. Her Bible supports English, Español, Português, Français, Deutsch, 简体中文 and 繁體中文.
